This post compares the OPERA Sentinel-1 disturbance product with the OPERA DIST-HLS products across a few specific sites.
In performing this comparison, we provide a perspective on the capabilities, strengths, and limits of the Sentinel-1 product.
The DIST-HLS has two disturbance products: the generic product and vegetation product.
The latter disturbance is the "official" product: it carefully models "vegetation cover" and delineates changes when a pixel undergoes 50% or more *vegetation cover loss*.
The former is the statistical/generic loss that *statistical* deviation of the recent acquisition with respect to the baseline of the images.
While both DIST-HLS disturbance products are validated carefully, there are no formal requirements for the latter.
The DIST-S1 product delineates disturbances with respect to *statistical* variations in a very similar fashion to DIST-HLS.
When comparing the products, we share all three.
